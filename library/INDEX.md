# Library of primary texts / Бібліотека першоджерел

Official texts of Ukrainian acts as plain text, for reviewers (human and AI). Texts of official documents are not subject to copyright (Закон України «Про авторське право і суміжні права», ст. 10). One folder per act, named by its id in `data/sources.csv`; group folders by issuer type.

Rules: the print view of zakon.rada.gov.ua (or the issuer's official PDF) is the reference; note the edition. Do not add texts whose publisher forbids reproduction (НД ТЗІ, standards): cite their identifiers instead.

To add an act: create `library/<type>/<id>/text.txt` with the URL and edition on the first line, then add a row below.
