# How to contribute

<p class="chapter-lead">GRC foundation is a non-commercial open project. Its quality depends on other GRC practitioners taking part: people who know the acts, apply the controls in practice and notice what is wrong or missing.</p>

## Where help is needed most

AI drafted most of the mappings between provisions of acts and controls. Each one becomes reliable only when a second person has checked it against the text of the act.

<div id="contrib-review"></div>

The order in which acts are mapped (laws first, then regulations sector by sector) and the progress of each stage are in the [mapping roadmap](#/about#mapping-roadmap).

## Ways to help

<ul class="help-list" id="ways">
<li><a href="#/controls"><b>Found a wrong mapping?</b><span>Use "Report an error" next to it in Controls: the form opens with the provision and the control filled in</span></a></li>
<li><a href="https://github.com/Ivan-Gokin/grc-foundation-ua/issues/new?template=new-or-changed-act.yml" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><b>An act is missing or has changed?</b><span>Tell us through a short GitHub form</span></a></li>

<li><a href="https://github.com/Ivan-Gokin/grc-foundation-ua/issues/new?template=report.yml" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><b>Missing something for your work?</b><span>Suggest it</span></a></li>
</ul>

Changes to data, texts or code are welcome as pull requests on [GitHub](https://github.com/Ivan-Gokin/grc-foundation-ua).
