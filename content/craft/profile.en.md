# Your profile

This is how the law sees your organisation: the categories that acts in force name directly, such as "bank", "owner of a critical infrastructure facility" or "processes personal data". If one is wrong or missing, adjust the profile: everything that follows depends on it.

The law is only part of the picture. Before you move on, write down for yourself, in a page or two:

- **what matters most:** the services customers or the state rely on, and what happens if they stop for an hour, a day, a week;
- **what you depend on:** IT outsourcing, cloud, software, telecom and energy suppliers;
- **who else expects something from you:** owners, key customers, partners, insurers;
- **the scope:** which units, processes, systems and locations your security management covers, and why. Leave nothing out that your most important services depend on.

*This is ISO/IEC 27001 clauses 4.1-4.3 and NIST CSF 2.0 GV.OC.*
