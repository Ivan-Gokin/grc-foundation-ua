# Who sets your rules

Your requirements come from two places. The **national cybersecurity system**: the Verkhovna Rada passes the laws, the Cabinet of Ministers sets the general model, and the SSSCIP (the State Service of Special Communications and Information Protection) writes the technical rules and checks them. And your **sector regulator**, if you have one: for a bank, the National Bank of Ukraine.

Below are the bodies whose acts bind you. For each one, record:

- **its role towards you:** sets rules, inspects, receives your reports, coordinates the response to an attack;
- **the channel:** whom to contact, how, and how fast an incident must be reported.

Keep these contacts in a shared place, not in one person's phone, and check them once a year.

*This is ISO/IEC 27001 control A.5.5 and NIST CSF 2.0 GV.OC-02.*
