# Your framework

A framework is the common language for your security measures: it names them, so you, your auditors and the regulator mean the same thing. This site supports two.

- **ISO/IEC 27001** is a management system standard with 93 controls. You can be certified against it, and customers and partners often ask for it. Its key document is the Statement of Applicability (SoA).
- **NIST CSF 2.0** describes outcomes in six functions: Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover. Its key document is a target profile. Ukraine's baseline cyber protection measures for critical infrastructure and public bodies are written in its terms.

They are not exclusive: you can be certified against ISO/IEC 27001 and report a NIST CSF profile to the regulator. Pick one as the main structure; the other can be linked to it later.
