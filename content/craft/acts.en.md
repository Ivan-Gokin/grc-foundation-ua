# Your laws and regulations

This is your register of requirements: what you must do, what you choose to do, and why.

- **Required by law.** Acts in force that name your profile. You cannot remove them. They are grouped into chains: a resolution or an order sits under the law it was issued under, and it is usually more specific than the law. Laws that only give a regulator its powers, and set no requirements for you, are hidden; "Show full chains" puts them back.
- **Recommended.** Acts that do not bind you but that regulators and good practice lean on. Accept the ones you will follow.
- **EU law worth following.** Not binding for organisations working only in Ukraine, but it shows where Ukrainian law is heading.
- **Your own.** What no public register holds: customer contracts, your parent company's rules, internal decisions. Add them as rows in the Acts sheet of your download.

Give every line an owner who will notice when it changes. The register is evidence for ISO/IEC 27001 control A.5.31 and NIST CSF 2.0 GV.OC-03.
